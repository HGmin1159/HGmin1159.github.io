I am a **Biostatistics PhD student at UNC-Chapel Hill**, working in statistical and computational genomics in the [Won Lab](https://www.wonlab.org/). I am interested in how genetic variation affects gene regulation and cellular function, and in statistical methods that help interpret genomic experiments.

## Single-cell genomics

My interests include cell-type-specific perturbation responses, differential gene expression, and gene programs in single-cell data. CROP-seq connects genetic perturbations with single-cell transcriptional readouts.

## Functional and regulatory genomics

I work with high-throughput functional experiments, including massively parallel reporter assays (MPRA), to study regulatory activity and the effects of genetic variants. My research interests span MPRA, single-cell MPRA, and CRISPR perturbation data.

## Statistical methodology

I am interested in statistical modeling and scalable computation for high-dimensional genomic data, including count data and differential expression in perturbation studies.
